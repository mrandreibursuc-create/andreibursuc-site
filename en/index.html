Background

Dr Andrei Bursuc

A specialist in physical medicine and rehabilitation, Dr Bursuc practises in Montréal at Clinique de physiatrie CDN. His practice includes electromyography, spasticity management and ultrasound-guided injections.

  • Doctor of MedicineUniversité de Montréal
  • Specialty in Physical Medicine and RehabilitationUniversité de Montréal
  • Master of Science in Experimental MedicineMcGill University
  • Subspecialization in neuromusculoskeletal ultrasound and neuro-orthopaedic and neuromuscular assessmentRaymond-Poincaré Hospital, Garches, France · AP-HP. Université Paris-Saclay
