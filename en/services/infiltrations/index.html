Physiatry consultations · Montréal

Ultrasound-guided injections

Ultrasound guidance makes it possible to see the needle and targeted structure during certain injections.

Which areas?

Depending on the indication: shoulder, elbow, wrist and fingers; hip, knee, ankle and foot; selected nerves and the sacroiliac joint. Cervical, thoracic and lumbar spine injections are not offered.

Which treatments?

Corticosteroid, viscosupplementation or platelet-rich plasma (PRP) may be considered according to the diagnosis, area and patient’s situation. Options are discussed after assessment; an injection is not automatic.

Consultation referral

Send a referral

A physician referral is required. The referring physician can complete the form and fax it to 514 558-8775.

After the referral is received, it is reviewed. If a consultation can be offered, the patient will be contacted about next steps.
