Physiatry in Montréal, EMG and appointments: answers to common patient questions.

What is a physiatrist?

A physiatrist is a physician specializing in physical medicine and rehabilitation. They assess muscle, joint and nervous system conditions, focusing on function, independence and quality of life.

Source : AAPM&R
When should I see a physiatrist?

A consultation may help with musculoskeletal pain, certain nerve conditions or functional difficulties following neurological illness. Your physician can advise whether physiatry is appropriate for you.

Source : AAPM&R

What is an electromyography (EMG) test for?

EMG and nerve conduction studies assess muscles and nerves. They can help investigate numbness or weakness, including nerve compression.

Source : Mayo Clinic
How should I prepare for an EMG?

Avoid skin creams before the test. Report medications, particularly blood thinners, and any pacemaker or bleeding disorder. Do not change treatment without medical instructions.

Source : Mayo Clinic
Is an EMG painful?

Electrical stimulation and the needle can be uncomfortable. Tell the examiner if you experience pain.

Source : Mayo Clinic
